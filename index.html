Hi! I'm Gojo Saturo

Gojo Satoru is the strongest jujutsu sorcerer alive, blessed with the rare Six Eyes and Limitless technique. His unmatched power and charismatic presence make him a living legend, capable of single-handedly changing the fate of the jujutsu world.

Gojo Satoru's Cursed Techniques

Limitless

The inherited technique of the Gojo family that grants control over space at an atomic level. By manipulating the concept of infinity, Gojo creates an impenetrable barrier that slows down approaching attacks infinitely, making him virtually untouchable in combat.

Six Eyes

An extremely rare ocular ability that allows Gojo to see cursed energy in incredible detail and process information at superhuman speeds. The Six Eyes drastically reduces the cursed energy cost of techniques, enabling him to maintain his powers indefinitely without exhaustion.

Hollow Purple

Gojo's most devastating attack, created by combining the repulsive force of Blue and the attractive force of Red. This imaginary mass obliterates everything in its path, leaving nothing but void as it tears through both matter and cursed energy.
